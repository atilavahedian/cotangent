Faster gradient handling, with the arithmetic intact.

The independent eager replication

Across 100 fresh paired seeds, time to the fixed validation target fell by 15.03% (99.5% CI, 13.50–16.56%). Fixed-budget training time fell by 16.97% (16.26–17.69%).

Mean full-test change: +0.00138 BPB (99.5% CI, −0.00451 to +0.00728). Its upper bound is below the predeclared 0.01-BPB tolerance. Ninety-nine pairs reached the target faster; one was slower. Fifty pairs had worse candidate test BPB. Every observation remains in the analysis.

03 / Method

Batch dispatch. Preserve the reduction.

Clipping first computes one norm per parameter, then combines those scalar norms. A flat reduction has the same real-arithmetic value, but floating-point additions and kernel selection can change its numerical result.

02Equal-length norm buckets

A layout chosen for the backend

Each equal-length bucket is viewed as B × (K/2) × 2. Reducing both trailing axes keeps the pinned MPS generic norm path. Scalar norms return to original parameter order before the final reduction. Short and odd lengths use native scalar fallbacks.

Consistent optimizer coordinates

Parameters, gradients and moment states share the same coordinate ordering. AdamW’s coordinatewise update commutes with that reindexing under the stated assumptions. All stacking, copying, concatenation and temporary buffers count in the measurements.

These controlled checks isolate numerical update behavior. Official training retains native embedding backward, whose accumulation can vary across independently executed runs. The implementation requires dense, contiguous, complete gradients and one optimizer hyperparameter group. Equivalence on other devices, versions, strides or execution paths needs separate verification.

Measured scope

One M5 Pro, PyTorch 2.14.1, byte language models from 3.35M to 19.28M parameters, and a reused WikiText-2 corpus. The saving concerns gradient handling and optimizer application. Full activations and derivative products remain, and temporary buffers add memory. Compiled comparisons use warm caches and per-step synchronization. Other Apple devices, CUDA, distributed training and larger model scales remain untested.
